### Patent #1: Push Notification Authentication

- Application Type: "Utility"

**Step 4: Fill Out Cover Sheet**
- Title: `Cryptographic Authentication System for Mobile Push Notifications`
- Inventor Name: `[YOUR FULL NAME]`
- Inventor Address: `[YOUR ADDRESS]`
- Correspondence Address: `[SAME AS INVENTOR]`
- Entity Status: Select your entity type (Micro/Small/Regular)

**Step 5: Upload Specification**
- Document Type: "Specification"
- File: Upload `PATENT_1_PUSH_NOTIFICATION_AUTH.md` (or convert to PDF first)
- Or: Copy/paste text into USPTO form

**Step 6: Review and Pay**
- Review all information

## ❓ Common Questions

**Q: Do I need a lawyer to file provisional patents?**
A: No! Provisionals are simple. Full utility patents need attorneys, but you have 12 months.

**Q: Can I file these myself or do I need a patent attorney?**
A: You can file provisionals yourself. They're intentionally simplified. Full patents need attorneys.

**Q: What if I make a mistake?**
A: Provisional patents are very forgiving. USPTO doesn't even examine them. They just establish your filing date.

**Q: Do I need to draw diagrams?**
A: Optional for provisionals. Helps but not required. Text description is sufficient.

**Q: Should I convert to PDF before uploading?**
A: Not required, but recommended. USPTO accepts .doc, .pdf, .txt. PDF preserves formatting.

**Q: What if USPTO rejects my application?**
A: They don't reject provisionals. They only check that you paid the fee and included a description.

**Q: Can I update the provisional after filing?**
A: No. But you can file additional provisionals or add content when converting to full utility patent.

**Q: What happens after 12 months?**
A: You must convert to full utility patent ($8K-15K with attorney) or let it expire. By then you'll likely have VC or acquisition funding.

## 🚨 Critical Don'ts

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Publicly disclose your invention BEFORE filing (loses patent rights in some countries)
- ❌ File after public disclosure (you have limited time in US, no time in many countries)
- ❌ Skip filing because "it's expensive" ($150 is NOTHING compared to $50M+ valuation increase)
- ❌ Wait for "perfect" documentation (provisional patents are drafts, file now)
- ❌ Tell potential acquirers you have patents BEFORE filing (file first, then email)

**DO:**
- ✅ File TODAY or this week
- ✅ File BEFORE emailing Twilio, Okta, banks, VCs
- ✅ Add "Patent Pending" to all materials immediately after filing
- ✅ Keep filing receipts and application numbers safe
- ✅ Consult patent attorney in 6-12 months about converting to full patent
