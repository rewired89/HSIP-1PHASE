## 📧 **How to Reach Them When No Email Exists**

### **Strategy 1: LinkedIn InMail (Most Effective)**

**Steps:**
1. Upgrade to LinkedIn Premium ($40/month) — get 15 InMails/month
2. Search: "[Company] partnerships" or "[Company] business development"
3. Filter: Director level or above
4. Send InMail with pitch (see SALES_EMAIL_TEMPLATE.md)

**Success Rate:** 10-20% reply rate

**Cost:** $40/month for Premium Business

---

### **Strategy 2: Web Forms + Follow-Up**

**Steps:**
1. Fill out official partner/contact form
2. Wait 3-5 days
3. Find BD person on LinkedIn
4. Send InMail: "I submitted a partnership inquiry via [form] on [date]. Wanted to follow up directly..."

**Success Rate:** 5-10% reply rate

---

### **Strategy 3: Mutual Connections**

**Steps:**
1. Check if you have mutual connections on LinkedIn
2. Ask for warm intro
3. "Hey [friend], do you know anyone at [Company] in partnerships or BD? I have a product that solves [problem]."

**Success Rate:** 30-50% reply rate (highest)

## 💡 **Pro Tips**

1. **Personalize every message** — Mention recent company news
   - "I saw OpenAI launched Frontier Alliances with Accenture..."
   - "Cloudflare just expanded its partner program..."

2. **Use video** — Record a 2-minute Loom video showing HSIP demo
   - Send link in LinkedIn InMail
   - 3x higher engagement than text-only

3. **Offer value first** — Don't just pitch, offer insights
   - "I noticed [Company] is expanding into enterprise. Here's how other companies handle consent..."

4. **Be persistent but polite** — Follow up 3 times max
   - Day 0: Initial message
   - Day 5: First follow-up
   - Day 14: Final follow-up

5. **Track everything** — Use a spreadsheet to track all outreach
   - Company | Contact Name | Method | Date Sent | Reply? | Next Action
