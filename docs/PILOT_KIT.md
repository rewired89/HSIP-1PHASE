# Paid pilot kit

Goal: 3 paid pilots at $500 each. A paid pilot is real demand; compliments are not.

## The offer (send this)

**60-day pilot, $500, credited toward the first 3 months of a paid plan.**
We connect one real system of yours (one stream), record its decisions as signed, chained proofs, and deliver one evidence pack your auditor, customer or counsel can verify offline with open code. Your decision content never leaves your systems; we only receive SHA-256 fingerprints.

Included: onboarding call, integration help (Python, Node, OpenTelemetry, LangChain, OpenAI Agents, GitHub Action, webhook relay), one evidence pack and printable report, a 30-minute review at day 45.
Not included: certification, legal advice, a guarantee that a regulator accepts it. A proof shows a record existed unaltered at a time, not that the decision was right.

## Who to approach first (in order)

1. AI vendors selling into banks, insurers or healthcare (their buyers ask for decision logs).
2. Forensics, e-discovery and managed security firms (chain of custody is already their product).
3. Provably-fair gaming and fantasy-sports operators (payout and RNG disputes).
4. Valuation and scoring model firms facing fair-lending or bias-audit exams.
5. AI governance consultants (as a channel: they would run it for many clients).

## Discovery questions (ask before pitching)

1. Who has asked, or will ask, you to show what your system decided? (regulator, customer, court, insurer)
2. What do you show them today, and what happens if they doubt it?
3. What would you need to see before trusting a young vendor with this? (audit, references, self-hosting, own keys)
4. Who signs off on a tool like this, and what is the budget path?
5. If we solved it, what would you pay per month? Say a number first, then ask.

## Success criteria (agree before starting)

- One system connected, at least 1,000 real decisions recorded.
- One evidence pack verified offline by someone outside the team.
- A written yes or no on continuing, with the reason.

## Rules for us

- Say the limits out loud: custodial keys by default (customer-held keys are available), no independent security audit yet, Stripe and email are new.
- Record every objection verbatim. The objection list decides what we build next.
- If a pilot customer will not name a buyer who asked for this, treat it as a free-tier user, not a pilot.
