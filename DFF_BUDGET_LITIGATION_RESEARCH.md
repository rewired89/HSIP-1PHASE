## BUDGET JUSTIFICATION

### Why This Budget?

**Personnel (65%):** Majority of work is skilled legal-technical research requiring simultaneous understanding of:
- EU privacy law (GDPR Articles 7, 30, 32)
- Cryptographic verification standards (Ed25519, SHA-256)
- Court evidence admissibility requirements
- Technical documentation for legal audiences

This specialized expertise justifies the allocation.

**Professional Services (20%):** Critical for research validity:
- Legal consultation ensures model briefs meet courtroom standards
- Cryptography review ensures technical accuracy for forensic examination
- Translation makes research accessible across EU jurisdictions
- Graphics help judges understand complex cryptography

**Research & Publication (10%):** Necessary infrastructure:
- Legal database access required for case law analysis
- Conference presentation disseminates findings to legal community
- Open-access publication ensures any affected individual can access research
- Physical materials for NGO partners who lack digital infrastructure

**Community Engagement (5%):** Ensures research addresses real litigation needs:
- Training materials empower NGOs to use HSIP evidence
- Interviews with affected individuals validate methodology
