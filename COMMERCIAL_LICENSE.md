## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can I try HSIP commercially before licensing?

**A:** Yes, we offer a 30-day evaluation period. Contact us to request evaluation access.

### Q: What if my startup grows beyond the startup license?

**A:** You can upgrade to an Enterprise license at any time. We'll prorate your existing license.

### Q: Can I use HSIP in open-source software?

**A:** Yes, if your project is non-commercial and open-source (OSI-approved license). If you monetize it (donations, paid support, hosted services), you need a commercial license.

### Q: Do I need a license for internal testing?

**A:** No, non-production testing and development is covered under the Commons License. Once you deploy to production for business purposes, you need a commercial license.

### Q: Can I modify HSIP for internal use?

**A:** Yes, all commercial licenses allow modifications for internal use. OEM licenses allow redistribution of modified versions.

### Q: What happens if my license expires?

**A:** You retain usage rights for the version you licensed, but lose access to updates, security patches, and support. You must renew to receive new versions.

### Q: Do you offer academic/research discounts?

**A:** Academic and research use is **free** under the Commons License. No commercial license needed for non-commercial research.

### Q: What if I'm a contractor building software for a client?

**A:** If the client will use HSIP commercially, they need a commercial license. If your consultancy deploys HSIP for multiple clients, you may need an OEM or partner license.
