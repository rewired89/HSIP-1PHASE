1. What HSIP Is

HSIP ("High Security Internet Protocol") is software that provides cryptographic identity management, consent records, message signing, verifiable credentials, and an audit trail. It is available as open-source software and as a hosted evaluation demo.

HSIP is a tool, not a compliance certification. Using HSIP does not make you compliant with any law or regulation. Whether HSIP's capabilities satisfy a specific requirement in your jurisdiction is a legal and compliance question — one you should answer with your own legal counsel.
